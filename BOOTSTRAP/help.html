We have developed the FAQs based on questions asked by our members and are sure that you will find answers to your questions here.

How to fill the online registration form on Mangalashtak.com ? keyboard_arrow_down

Go to the online registration form. Fill out the required (marked * symbol) information as accurately and click on the Register button. We will send your user id and password to your email id, and you will become a Registered member of Mangalashtak.com . It is absolutely FREE of cost!

How much money do I have to give to Register on Mangalashtak.com for membership? keyboard_arrow_down

It is absolutely FREE of cost. Submit your profile is absolutely free of cost. A nominal fee has to be paid to view the contact details of others.

When does the registration get activated? keyboard_arrow_down

Once we get your registration form and your payment receipts are confirmed, your service will be activated and you can start using our services.

What is a Mangalashtak.com paid Membership? keyboard_arrow_down

Membership with which you are entitled to view the others profile on Mangalashtak.com is entirely FREE of cost. However, to view the contact details of other profiles on Mangalashtak.com there is a minimal service charge. This service is not available for non-paying FREE members of Mangalashtak.com .

What are the benefits of Registering on Mangalashtak.com ? keyboard_arrow_down

  • You have to be Registered member for searching and viewing the profiles.
  • You have to be a paid member for viewing the contact details of profiles.
  • You can add information about yourself and your family - FREE.

I already Registered but cannot login to my Mangalashtak.com account. Why? keyboard_arrow_down

When you done Register on Mangalashtak.com you will get an USERNAME and PASSWORD after registration. That contains your Mangalashtak.com "userid (Matrimony ID)" and "password". If you have not received for whatever reasons, then your Mangalashtak.com account is still inactive. Then feel free to contact us and we will send your userid and password.

I didn't receive an email. Now what do I do? keyboard_arrow_down

Sometimes emails get lost. Or it is possible that you may have entered an incorrect email address by mistake. You can email us with the Name, username and email address that you used to Register, and we will go through it and get back to you as soon as possible.

What are the benefits of the Mangalashtak.com paid Membership? keyboard_arrow_down

The Mangalashtak.com paid Membership allows you to view the contact details of other Registered members and initiate contact with them. As a free member the applicant is allowed to search and view other member's profile but you cannot view their contact details.
